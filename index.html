Show open palm for 3 seconds to lock/unlock
Hold open palm for over 5 seconds to show all gesture controls

Lock/unlock: show an open palm for 3 seconds.

Turn: hold thumb and index finger wide apart and twist your hand like a big dial.

Finer movement: bring thumb and finger closer together.

Pause: touch thumb and finger together. This also allows you to reposition your hand and keep turning.

Moving your hand out of view leaves the wheel where it is.

To add titles and information, put a spreadsheet in the folder (CSV, Excel or LibreOffice) with one column that
          contains the filename of each image. You'll be asked which column to use as the title and up to four more to show under the enlarged image.

        
Are.na channel… loads the images from a public Are.na channel. Paste the channel's link, e.g.
          are.na/your-name/your-channel. Reloading the page returns to the placeholders.
